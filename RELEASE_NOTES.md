# Life DB 1.0.0

**Life DB 1.0 — the notebook.** A private, offline habit tracker and journal that replaces your paper notebook. Everything stays on your device, encrypted.

- **Habits** of four kinds (yes/no, number, duration, rating), with targets, schedules and rest days
- **Daily view** with every habit and the day's journal entries, fully usable by keyboard
- **Monthly view** laid out like a paper tracker, with a summary row
- **Journal** in Markdown, several entries a day, saved as you type
- **Categories and tags** shared across habits and journal entries
- **Your data, safe**: encrypted on disk, automatic daily backups, restore, and signed in-app updates

Coming in 1.1: goals and milestones, life events, analytics and search.
