# Life DB 1.1.0

**Life DB 1.1 completes V1.** Goals, life events, analytics and search join the notebook. Your existing data stays exactly as it is: this update changes nothing in your database.

- **Goals**: track progress by milestones, a percentage, or a count toward a target (7 of 20 books). Link the habits that help, and pick months to list a goal under that month's grid.
- **Life events**: note a day or a range, like a trip. Events show at the top of each day they cover and as ✦ markers in the monthly grid.
- **Analytics**: each habit's stats for a week, month, year or any range, next to the previous period's, with a chart. Plus a summary of every goal.
- **Search**: one box for your journal, habits, goals, events and tags. Press **Ctrl+K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) from anywhere. "fren" finds "French", accents and capitals don't matter, and you can filter by type, dates, category or tag.
- The daily view shows your goals with the status of their habits for the day, and each habit's page now has its stats and chart.
